Slaughterhouse

Slaughter House is a spine-chilling survival horror game that plunges you into the depths of a decaying slaughterhouse, where your mission is not just to survive—but to destroy it. You take on the role of a peace-loving hippie, driven not by a sense of justice, but by a dark desire to impress the notorious Charles Manson. What starts as a twisted quest for approval soon turns into a desperate fight for survival.

The slaughterhouse has become a breeding ground for unspeakable mutations. Twisted creatures, born of the facility's dark past, now roam the halls, turning your mission into a terrifying struggle for survival.

As you explore the twisted corridors, you'll uncover the horrifying secrets that led to the slaughterhouse's downfall. The deeper you venture, the more you realize that destroying this place might not be as simple as you thought. Will you have the courage to face the nightmares within and succeed in your mission or will the Slaughter House claim yet another victim?

Lead Narrative: Lore, collectibles...
Movement system: ABP, Logic (Unreal Engine)
AI System for enemy creatures, their movements, attacks and behavior
Combat system: Diverse actions such as attacking, bullet managing, etc
Unreal Engine 5 for gameplay systems and overall development
Blender for 3D modeling, character rigging and animation
Custom assets: level design, character setup and environment props
Resident Evil soundtrack integrated to enhance immersion and pacing
Adobe Photoshop for hand-painted textures and UI elements
Project exported and published on Itch.io for public release

This project was a collaborative effort. While I focused on programming, AI, and narrative design, other team members contributed with 3D modeling, animation, sound design, and QA.

Designing the narrative through environmental storytelling and optional collectibles posed an interesting challenge in terms of lore development. Implementing the combat system gave me the opportunity to deepen my work with behavior trees and blackboards, enhancing my ability to create more dynamic and challenging enemy AI.
